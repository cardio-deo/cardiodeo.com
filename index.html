01

Exercise Yourself
in Godliness.

1 Timothy 4:7

The Greek word is gymnaze, the root of gymnasium. Paul was not speaking in metaphor: the soul, like the body, is trained.

Cardio Deo is your daily training log for the interior life. Like a fitness tracker, you bring your own equipment and the app provides a structured plan (Bible, prayer beads, etc.).

From Browse All, choose your exercises, each with tips for Proper Form.
