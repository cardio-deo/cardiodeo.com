A gym for the soul, and a few practical notes.

Cardio Deo  ·  Version 1.0.2

Cardio Deo keeps your practice on your device. Most questions come down to backing that data up, and getting it onto a new phone if you need to.

Questions

How do I back up my data?

Open the Me tab, then choose Export Backup. The app writes a JSON file with your journal, practice log, routines, reminder schedules, psalter progress, and custom activities. Save that file somewhere you can find it later, such as a folder you already back up.

What if I delete the app without exporting?

The data stored on that device is removed with the app. Because we never receive a copy, we cannot restore it. Export a backup first if you want to keep your record.

How do I move to a new device?

On the old device, export a backup and keep the file. Install Cardio Deo on the new device, open the Me tab, and choose Import Backup. After the import, restart the app so your data appears.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for, and nothing to sign into.

Why did the app ask for notification permission?

Only if you set a reminder. Those reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not push notifications from a server.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

It is on this site: cardiodeo.com/privacy.
