Privacy Policy

Your interior life stays yours.

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers the Cardio Deo app and this website, cardiodeo.com. Cardio Deo is a Catholic and Ignatian prayer app published by Weil Consulting LLC.

The short version

Your practice stays on your device. Cardio Deo does not use accounts, and Weil Consulting does not operate a server that receives your journal, prayer log, intentions, or any other practice data.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a local record of your spiritual practice, including:

  • your practice log and psalter progress
  • journal entries
  • prayer intentions
  • routines and reminder schedules
  • custom activities
  • a few settings, such as whether you have finished the orientation

This information never has to leave your phone or tablet for the app to work. It is encrypted at rest on the device with AES-256-GCM. You can delete it by deleting the app, after exporting a backup if you want to keep a copy.

What we do not collect

We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or payment software in the app. We do not receive your practice data, and we do not sell or share personal information, because the app does not send us any.

Network use

The app’s own code does not call a backend or send your data over the network. A few features include links to other websites — for example, daily lectionary readings, hymn melodies, and reference pages. If you tap one of those links, your device opens it in the system browser, and that site’s privacy policy applies. We do not receive a record of the visit.

Reminders you schedule are local notifications on your device. They are not push notifications and do not use a notification service we control.

Permissions

The app may ask for permission to send you the reminders you set, and it uses the system share and file-picker tools when you export or import a backup. The Android build also declares an Internet permission, which comes with the framework the app is built on; the app does not use it to transmit your practice data.

Backups you make

From the Me tab you can export a backup file and later import it. That file is a copy of your practice data in JSON form. You choose where it is stored and who can see it. If you share it, or save it to a cloud folder, that is under your control, not ours.

If you delete the app without exporting a backup, the data on that device is removed with it. We cannot restore it.

Device and platform backups

If you use a device or platform backup service, such as Android Backup or iCloud, a copy of the app’s local data may be included in that backup. That copy is controlled by you and by the platform, not by Weil Consulting.

Purchases

If you buy Cardio Deo through Google Play or the Apple App Store, the store handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details.

This website

These pages do not use cookies or analytics. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary server logs (for example, IP address and the page requested) in the course of serving the site.

If you write to us

If you email support@cardiodeo.com, we will use your address and your message to reply. We do not use that correspondence for marketing.

Children

Cardio Deo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, so there is nothing for us to delete from our systems. If a child uses the app, any practice data stays on the device, under the control of the person who owns it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
